# Digit sum of a huge power (skipped)

Skipped. [Project Euler 16](https://projecteuler.net/problem=16) asks for the digit sum of
2^1000, a number about 302 digits long, where `Int64` stops at 19. Arbitrary-precision
integers are tracked under
[kantord/toylang#112](https://github.com/kantord/toylang/issues/112) and deliberately
unscheduled. See the [spoiler warning](00-spoiler-warning.md).
