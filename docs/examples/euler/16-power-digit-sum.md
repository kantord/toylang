# Digit sum of a huge power (skipped)

Skipped. [Project Euler 16](https://projecteuler.net/problem=16) asks for the digit sum of
2^1000, a number about 302 digits long -- nothing `Int` sized could hold even one multiplication
step of the way there, let alone the finished value. See
[kantord/toylang#38](https://github.com/kantord/toylang/issues/38) and the
[spoiler warning](00-spoiler-warning.md).
