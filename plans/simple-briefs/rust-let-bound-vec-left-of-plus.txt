Board row `rust-let-bound-vec-left-of-plus`. The Rust backend (src/emit_rs.rs) emits a `let`
without `mut` when the let-bound or destructured Vec is the LEFT operand of `+`, so rustc
rejects it (E0596, `t_N.extend(...)` on an immutable binding).

Minimal repro:
    fn f({acc}: {acc: Vec<Int>}) -> Vec<Int> = acc + [1]
called as `f({acc: [0]})`. `p.acc + [1]` and `[1] + acc` already work.

Where to look (do not survey the repo): `concat_owned` at src/emit_rs.rs:1539 emits
`t.extend(...)` on the owned left operand; `owned()` at :1530 decides via `self.mutables`
which locals are treated as owned/mutable. The bug is that a local reaches `concat_owned` as
owned while the `let` that binds it (grep `mutables` in src/emit_rs.rs for where the set is
filled and where `let mut` is printed) was not marked `mut`. Fix by making the binding `mut`
whenever `concat_owned` will mutate it -- the smallest change wins; do not restructure.

of a neighbouring tests/corpus/*.yaml case) so all backends agree on the result `[0, 1]`.
