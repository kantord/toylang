## Background (why this matters)

The `drive` skill (`.claude/skills/drive/SKILL.md`) describes, in its dispatch section (step 3 of
"The loop"), the three verbs a tick uses on a non-GREEN dispatch outcome: (a) a narrower redispatch
per the agent's own suggestion, (b) a decide-row escalation when the report says this is a scope
problem, (c) a HARNESS DEFECT escalation. Verbs (a) and (b) are mutually exclusive for a single row:
choosing (a) -- redispatching narrower -- only makes sense once any decide row that was gating the
original row's scope has actually been resolved one way or another. Today the skill text does not
say what to do with that gating decide row when a tick picks verb (a) instead of leaving it open.

The failure mode this guards against: a tick redispatches a row narrower (verb a), the row lands,
but a decide row that was created to gate the ORIGINAL (wider) scope is left sitting open in
`plans/board.yaml`/a forest round with `needs` pointing at a row that has already moved on. The
decide row becomes a stale question nobody will ever act on, or -- worse -- silently keeps gating
something it no longer describes.
