## Background (why this matters)

`transpose` (built by `tensor-transpose-build`, archived done in `plans/board-archive.yaml`) only
emits on the Go and Rust backends. The other five backends (JS, Python, Lua, jq, LLVM) hit
`unreachable!` when a program calls `transpose` and gets emitted for one of them. Self-originated
from the 2026-09-18 status audit (`plans/status-audit-2026-09-18.md`).

Look at how `tensor-transpose-build` implemented Go and Rust for the exact semantics to replicate:
`git log --all --oneline --grep transpose` and check the archived row / its landing commit diff for
the Go and Rust emitter arms as the reference implementation.
